Discover Bulawayo

Known as the City of Kings and Queens, Bulawayo is Zimbabwe's second-largest city and a centre of history, culture, commerce, and tourism. Discover the attractions that make Bulawayo and its surrounding region unique.

Why Bulawayo?

Bulawayo is an important commercial and industrial centre in Zimbabwe. Its railway heritage, cultural institutions, tourism attractions, and strategic location provide opportunities for businesses, investors, and visitors.

The city is also a gateway to attractions such as Matobo National Park and Khami Ruins, making it an important destination for heritage tourism.
